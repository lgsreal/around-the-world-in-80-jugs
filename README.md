## Mission

AI is changing how software is written, but it does not replace the engineer. **Around the World in 80 JUGs** exists to help developers become better software engineers in the age of AI by strengthening the fundamentals that never go out of style:

- **Software design** — modeling, abstraction, and code that is easy to change
- **Java** — the language, the platform, and its ecosystem
- **Testing** — confidence, feedback loops, and safe refactoring
- **Architecture** — trade-offs, boundaries, and long-term maintainability
- **Debugging** — understanding systems instead of guessing
- **Requirements** — asking the right questions before writing code
- **Critical thinking** — evaluating answers, including the ones an AI gives you

The project is also a celebration of the **global Java community**. Every stop on the journey is a different JUG, country, and culture — and a chance to share knowledge, learn from local communities, and connect developers across borders.

## The Talks

The main talk is about **software engineering fundamentals**. JUGs can also choose one of the related topics below — each one builds on the same fundamentals.

### Main talk: How to Become a Better Software Engineer in the Age of AI

**Abstract (ready to copy):**

> AI assistants can generate code in seconds, but writing code was never the hardest part of software engineering. Understanding problems, designing solutions, testing behavior, debugging failures, and making sound architectural decisions still depend on the engineer. In this session, Luiz Real explores how developers can grow into better software engineers in the age of AI by strengthening their fundamentals. You will learn why AI is a powerful tool rather than a replacement for engineering judgment, how strong fundamentals help you guide and review AI-generated work, and practical habits you can apply right away in your day-to-day work.

**What to expect:**

- **Practical** — concrete habits and examples developers can apply immediately.
- **Community-oriented** — an open conversation with plenty of room for questions and local perspectives.
- **For all levels** — valuable for students and junior developers as well as experienced Java engineers and tech leads.
- **In English** — all sessions are delivered in English.

## For JUG Organizers

Would you like **Around the World in 80 JUGs** to stop at your Java User Group? Luiz would love to visit your community — virtually (or maybe in person, who knows?).

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Suggested talk** | *How to Become a Better Software Engineer in the Age of AI* — or one of the [other topics](#other-topics) (Refactoring, AI-First SDLC, Career) |
| **Duration** | Flexible — suggested 45–60 minutes plus Q&A (adjustable to your meetup format) |
| **Format** | Online talk or fireside chat, optionally with a guest co-speaker from the Java community |
| **Streaming** | Your JUG's usual platform (e.g., Zoom, Google Meet, StreamYard, YouTube Live). Please share the link and a quick tech check slot in advance. Recording and publishing are welcome if your JUG allows it. |
| **Time zones** | Sessions can be scheduled across time zones — please propose a few dates/times in your local time zone with the UTC offset. |
| **Language** | English only. |

**How to invite Luiz:**

1. [Open an issue](https://github.com/lgsreal/around-the-world-in-80-jugs/issues/new) titled `JUG invitation: <Your JUG name>`.
2. Include your JUG name, city/country, proposed dates and times (with time zone), expected audience, streaming platform, and organizer contact.
3. Optionally, suggest a guest co-speaker — for example, a JUG leader or Java Champion from your JUG.
4. Luiz will reply to confirm details and add your JUG to the [journey table](#journey--episodes).

> 👉 **Open an issue or [contact Luiz](#contact) to schedule a session.**

## For Potential Guests

Are you a member of the Java community — a Java Champion, JUG leader, speaker, author, or practitioner — who cares about software engineering fundamentals? Join a stop on the journey as a **co-speaker or guest**.

The talk is usually delivered as a **live discussion / conversation**, so you don't need to prepare fancy slides — but feel free to use visual resources if you want. You're also welcome to share a call to action (e.g., your book, community, or project), but the goal is not selling things or turning the talk into a commercial one.

**Topics that fit the mission:**

- Software design and clean, maintainable code
- Modern Java and the JVM ecosystem
- Testing strategies and test-driven development
- Software architecture and trade-offs
- Debugging and observability
- Requirements, communication, and working with product teams
- Critical thinking and reviewing AI-generated code
- Career growth and learning in the age of AI
- Building and growing developer communities

> 👉 **Interested?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/lgsreal/around-the-world-in-80-jugs/issues/new) titled `Guest proposal: <Your name>` with a short bio, topic ideas, and preferred time zone — or [contact Luiz](#contact) directly.

## Contributing / Updating the Repo

The website is a [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) site deployed to GitHub Pages by [`.github/workflows/hugo.yml`](.github/workflows/hugo.yml) on every push to `main`. Its content lives in `data/`:

| File | Content |
|------|---------|
| `data/journey.yaml` | Every stop (date, JUG, location, topic, guests, link, status). The site's counter is computed from it. |
| `data/talks.yaml` | Main talk (first entry) and other topics, with abstracts. |
| `data/ports.yaml` | Home city and coordinates of each JUG, used to place its pin on the journey map. |
| `data/guests.yaml` | Host and guests. |
| `data/links.yaml` | Resources and contact links. |

Preview locally with `hugo server` and open http://localhost:1313/around-the-world-in-80-jugs/.

**Maintainer workflow after each session:**

1. Add or update the stop in `data/journey.yaml`. For a new JUG, also add its home city to `data/ports.yaml` so it appears on the map.
2. Update this README: the **progress counter** at the top (badge, `X / 80` text, and progress bar) and the **[Journey / Episodes](#journey--episodes)** table.
3. Add any new guests to `data/guests.yaml` and the **[Guests](#guests)** table.
4. Add new books or recommended materials to `data/links.yaml` and **[Resources](#resources)**.
5. Close the related invitation or guest issue with a link to the recording.

**Possible future structure** (create only when there is content to add):

- `slides/` — slide decks for each version of the talk
- `sessions/` — per-session notes, links, and Q&A highlights
- `assets/` — images, logos, and banners (Hugo also uses `assets/` for processed files)

Suggestions and corrections are welcome — feel free to [open an issue](https://github.com/lgsreal/around-the-world-in-80-jugs/issues) or a pull request.
