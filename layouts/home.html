Chapter I

The Mission

AI is changing how software is written, but it does not replace the engineer. This voyage exists to strengthen the fundamentals that never go out of style — and to celebrate the global Java community, one JUG, country, and culture at a time.

  • Software design code that is easy to change
  • Design patterns proven solutions, used with judgment
  • Java the language and its ecosystem
  • Testing confidence and safe refactoring
  • Architecture trade-offs and boundaries
  • Security safe by design, not an afterthought
  • Debugging understanding, not guessing
  • Requirements the right questions first
  • Critical thinking including about AI answers
  • Career growing your skills, visibility and impact
